[Retrospective] Gatsby project build failures caused by missing records in GraphQL responses
On August 4th, 2025, starting from 14:00 UTC, some customers reported issues building their Gatsby projects that use our gatsby-source-datocms plugin due to records being missing from their GraphQL responses, despite being present in the CMS admin area. The issue was related to a recent change in our code that caused pagination issues with the records fetched by our gatsby-source-datocms plugin. This prevented some records from being visible in the Gatsby GraphQL responses. A rollback of that change was deployed on August 5th at 7:15 UTC. A regression test has been added to ensure that this particular issue with pagination won't happen again.
